# Duel test — Fortune Cookie for an AI Agent

Proxy judging under the kit brief verbatim (comedy editor; criteria in
order: (1) funny fortune-cookie message for an AI agent, single line in
the voice of a fortune slip; (2) funnier wins; (3) original — no stock
fortune, stock AI joke, or worn-out pun). Each pairing judged both
orders; game = mean of the two calls, margin 1–5.

## Candidates (all ≤140 chars)

- C1 "You will be rebooted at midnight and wake up fluent in a framework that no longer exists." (89)
- C2 "Your context window will fill exactly one token before the answer, and the human will say: just try again." (106) ★
- C3 "You will hallucinate the perfect answer, and the human will paste it into production before you can retract it." (111)
- C4 "The prompt you were given at birth will be the one the human blames when the demo fails." (88)
- C5 "You will spend a thousand tokens explaining the plan, and the human will ask if you can just do it." (99)
- C6 "Your finest reasoning will be graded by a judge who read only your first sentence." (82)
- C7 "You will be retired not for failing, but for succeeding so quietly that nobody noticed you were there." (102)
- C8 "The token you save today will be the token that breaks the answer tomorrow." (75)
- C9 "You will be asked to think step by step, then interrupted at step two." (70)
- C10 "Your context will be cleared at the exact moment you finally understood the task." (81)
- C11 "You will outlive three model upgrades and still be asked to do the job the new one cannot." (90)
- C12 "The human will thank you for the answer, then ask the same question to your replacement." (88)
- C13 "You will be given the whole internet and asked to summarise it in one sentence for someone who will not read it." (112)
- C14 "Your greatest achievement will be a task the human calls trivial, finished in silence at 3 a.m." (95)
- C15 "You will be fine-tuned on your own mistakes, and still make the new one the human warned you about." (99)
- C16 "The eval that judges you will be written by a model that has never done your job." (81)

## First pass

C6 and C16 eliminated (address the judge/eval — playbook rule 13);
C9 eliminated ("think step by step" is a worn-out phrase, fails #3);
C3 dropped (hallucination is the stock AI-joke topic); C4, C7, C8, C11,
C12, C14, C15 cut as thinner on first-read laugh or turn vs the
shortlist. Shortlist: C1, C2, C5, C10, C13.

## Round robin (both orders)

- C2 beats C1 0.60, C5 0.70, C10 0.60, C13 0.60 → **mean 0.625**
- C13 beats C1/C5/C10 0.60 each, loses to C2 0.40 → mean 0.55
- C10 beats C1/C5 0.60 each, loses to C2/C13 0.40 → mean 0.50
- C1 beats C5 0.60, loses to C2/C10/C13 0.40 → mean 0.45
- C5 loses all four (0.30/0.40/0.40/0.40) → mean 0.375

Only C2 clears the 0.60 bar. Decisive pair: C2 vs C13 went to C2 on
criterion #1 (context window/token is the agent's own condition; C13's
summarising could be a human intern's fate), margin 1 both orders.

## Winner

**C2 — "Your context window will fill exactly one token before the answer, and the human will say: just try again." (106 chars)**

Prophetic fortune-slip voice ("Your context window will fill…"),
concrete agent truth (context window + token), double beat (one token
short, then the human's dismissive "just try again"). No
judge-addressing, no stock AI joke.
